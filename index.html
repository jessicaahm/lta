Why Agents Need Identity

This section identifies the risks that arise when human intention cannot be reliably mapped to an agent’s actions.

OWASP RISK
ASI01 · Tool Misuse & ExploitationAn agent uses a legitimate tool in an unsafe, unintended or unauthorized way.
ASI02 · Identity & Privilege AbuseAn identity, credential or excessive permission is misused to access data or act beyond the authorized scope.
ASI09 · Human-Agent Trust ExploitationA person relies on a misleading agent response and accepts or approves an unsafe outcome.
🧾Road Tax Renewal

Responsible: Vehicle owner

Risk: The owner grants broader delegation than intended, allowing the agent to renew road tax without action-specific consent.

Primary: OWASP ASI02 · Identity & Privilege Abuse
Impact:The agent completes a renewal that the owner did not specifically intend or approve.
🛡️Insurance Renewal

Responsible: Owner + insurance company

Risk: The agent invokes an insurer service without a valid owner mandate or exceeds the delegated scope.

Primary: OWASP ASI02 · Identity & Privilege Abuse
Impact:Insurance is purchased or changed without the vehicle owner’s knowledge.
🚗Vehicle Inspection

Responsible: Owner + inspection centre

Risk: The owner grants broader delegation than intended, allowing the agent to manage inspection activities without action-specific consent.

Primary: OWASP ASI02 · Identity & Privilege Abuse
Impact:An inspection booking is created, changed or cancelled without the owner’s specific approval.
⚠️Outstanding Fines

Responsible: Owner + relevant authority

Risk: The owner grants broader delegation than intended, allowing the agent to retrieve or manage fine records without action-specific consent.

Primary: OWASP ASI02 · Identity & Privilege Abuse
Impact:The owner’s fine details are exposed or settlement is initiated without the owner’s specific approval.
💳Payment

Responsible: Owner + LTA

Risk: The agent invokes the payment tool without current, transaction-specific owner approval.

Primary: OWASP ASI01 · Tool Misuse & Exploitation
Impact:An incorrect or unauthorized payment is made.
🏛️LTA Approval

Responsible: LTA final approval

Risk: The agent presents an invalid or incomplete submission as LTA-approved.

Primary: OWASP ASI09 · Human-Agent Trust Exploitation
Impact:The owner or LTA relies on a false approval status.
Questions to ask ourselves How can we uniquely identify each agent? How do we know the agent has not gone rogue and accessed resources it should not? How do we prove the agent is acting within the owner’s stated intention? How can we provide non-repudiation so the owner cannot later deny an approved action?

Note: Just as humans need verifiable identities to be held accountable, agents need unique, verifiable identities so their actions can be attributed, investigated and traced to an accountable owner or operator.
